# Scene lighting variants

These WebP assets were created with the built-in image generation tool by editing the corresponding background in `assets/background/`. The generated images were resized to approximately 1280 × 720 and encoded as WebP for the game. The original scene images remain in place.

The edits should keep camera framing and the position of interactive objects recognizable, because the game uses fixed hotspot coordinates.

## Night variants

Common prompt:

> Use case: lighting-weather. Edit the supplied in-game background to show the same location at night in a realistic Vietnamese village horror adventure. Keep the exact camera viewpoint, image framing, architecture, trees, paths, bridge and all important objects in exactly the same screen positions because invisible game hotspots depend on them. Change sunlight and sky to dim blue night with subtle warm practical lights where appropriate, while keeping enough visibility for gameplay. No monsters, apparitions, text, interface, watermark, new structures or changed composition. Photorealistic, restrained.

Additional instructions:

- `BG01-night.webp`: At the left foreground, keep the elderly woman seated at the tea table and the entire tea setup in their original positions. The village gate must remain centered and unchanged. Reduce incidental passers-by and market bustle so it feels like a quiet late evening, while preserving the woman.
- `BG02-night.webp`: Keep both rows of old houses, the central lane and the large ceramic jar at the far right in the exact same positions. Make the market largely closed and quiet after dark; remove incidental crowds while retaining structures and stalls.
- `BG03-night.webp`: Keep the dirt path, canal at right, wooden bridge, bamboo on the left and rice fields in the exact same positions. Remove the visible bright sun and daytime workers; depict the empty fields under a dark blue night sky.
- `BG04-night.webp`: Keep the bamboo bridge, its broken plank, water channel and massive banyan at right in exactly the same positions. Replace the orange sunset with a dark blue night sky. The path and bridge must stay recognizable.
- `BG45-night.webp`: Keep the temple, frangipani tree, courtyard, wooden table and the dish of coins exactly where they are. Use a peaceful night atmosphere with soft lantern light. No scary figure or sudden horror effect.
